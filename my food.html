Biryani
A Taste of Happiness!

Biryani is a popular and delicious rice dish made with fragrant basmati rice, spices, vegetables, and meat or eggs. It is loved for its rich aroma, flavorful taste, and beautiful presentation. Biryani is prepared by cooking rice with a mixture of aromatic spices and other ingredients. Different regions have their own styles of biryani, each with a unique taste and preparation method 😀

Ingredients
  • Basmati rice
  • onions
  • tomato
  • ginger
  • species
  • chicken
  • currry leaves

Receipe

  1. Clean the rice and soak it for 30 min
  2. Take into bowl
  3. roast the onions and spieces for 5 min
  4. add the rice into bowl
  5. boil for 30 min
  6. add chicken pieces

Food information
Food type Biryani
Type Main course
Preparation time 30 minutes
Cooking time 45 min
Medium difficuly
